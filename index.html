Privacy Policy – Inventory hub

Last updated: 11 November 2026

Inventory hub ("the app") is a warehouse and store app published by its independent developer ("we"). It lets the staff of a company that uses Microsoft Dynamics 365 Finance & Operations ("D365FO") receive, ship, transfer and count stock from an Android device.

This policy explains what information the app handles, where it goes, and the choices you have.

Who is responsible for your data

The app is used by companies ("your employer") for their own staff. Your employer runs its own D365FO system, creates your user in it, and decides how the information the app sends there is used and kept. For that information your employer is the data controller; please contact them first about it.

We provide the app. The app does not send your information to any server of ours, and we do not sell it or use it for advertising.

The app does not collect your location, contacts, messages, call logs, files or browsing history, and it shows no advertising.

Google and Microsoft services in the app

  • Microsoft Entra ID (login.microsoftonline.com) gives the app a sign-in token for your employer's D365FO. The app identifies itself to it, not you.
  • Google ML Kit reads barcodes on the device. Google's ML Kit SDK may send Google basic technical information, such as device model, Android version, app version and performance figures, to keep the service working. It does not send camera images. See Google's ML Kit terms and privacy information.
  • Google Play Integrity, used for the licence check, asks Google to confirm that the app and device are genuine. Google handles this under its own privacy policy: https://policies.google.com/privacy.

Information kept on the device

Your session, work you have not sent yet (for example a count taken while offline) and a short technical log are kept on the device, encrypted where they are sensitive. They are deleted when they have been sent, when they are no longer needed, or when the app is uninstalled.

Security

All traffic between the app and D365FO or Microsoft is encrypted (HTTPS/TLS). Sensitive values on the device are kept in Android's encrypted storage.

How long information is kept

Information sent to D365FO is kept according to your employer's own rules. The app keeps nothing longer than it needs on the device; see "Information kept on the device".

Your choices and rights

  • You can choose not to send feedback, screenshots or logs, and not to use the camera or fingerprint unlock.
  • Your user is created and removed by your employer. To see, correct or delete information in D365FO, contact your employer's administrator.
  • For anything about the app itself, contact us (below).

Children

The app is for company staff only and is not directed at children.
